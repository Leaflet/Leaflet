Live day/night shading with a smooth ramp through civil, nautical and astronomical twilight, painted per tile as a `GridLayer` rather than as one hard-edged polygon. Includes a companion layer that masks city-lights imagery (NASA Black Marble) to the night side, so cities stop glowing in daylight over any basemap.
